Record your own voice and game sound. This guide is not evidence of a test already performed.

- 0:00–0:25: Show menu music, then enter the game and allow the crossfade to finish. “This is Tuman. I added two music tracks and eleven sound events.”
- 0:25–1:05: Move, shoot, collect, take damage, defeat an enemy. Show both wolf and ghost warnings before strikes. “The warning starts when the enemy begins its wind-up, so I can react before damage.” Demonstrate UI click and victory/defeat sounds.
- 1:05–1:30: Pause/resume, open settings. “The Paused snapshot lowers and muffles the music. Music and sound effects have separate sliders.”
- 1:30–2:00: Change volumes on the phone, fully close/force-stop the APK, relaunch and show the restored settings and audible levels.
- 2:00–2:40: Show AudioMixer Master/Music/Tracks/SFX, Normal/Paused and exposed faders. “GameSession controls GameAudio. GameAudio owns two music sources and reusable effect voices. EnemyAI, health, shooting, pickups and UI send events. SoundLibrary stores the clips; SaveService stores the volumes.”
- End: Show README licenses and DECISIONS. Explain a real problem/fix in your own words. “The sounds come from Kenney and OpenGameArt under CC0. Logarithmic volume makes the slider useful; unscaled time lets the pause transition finish.”
